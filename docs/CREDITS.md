## 4K texture pack

By **PlatinumMimic** (platinummin on Buy Me a Coffee, **u/Calm-Consideration41** on
Reddit), made for PCSX2 on the American PS2 version (SLUS-21168). The textures
were processed in chaiNNer to stay sharp at high resolutions without changing the art
style. The author distributes the pack for free, as a personal project, and accepts optional
donations. They also made the 4K pack for *Lament of Innocence*.

- Author's page (download): <https://buymeacoffee.com/platinummin/castlevania-ps2-4k-texture-packs>
- r/PCSX2 post: <https://www.reddit.com/r/PCSX2/comments/1sry8qk/castlevania_curse_of_darkness_4k_texture_pack/>
- Author's screenshots: <https://imgur.com/a/bl8rsoJ>

## What this project did with that material

- Converted texts, images and audio from the PS2 patch to the Xbox version's formats, with
  scripts that run on the user's own ISOs (`tools/ps2_*.py`). See
  [TRANSLATION.md](TRANSLATION.md).
- The game, translation, dub and HD pack files are not distributed in
  this repository.
