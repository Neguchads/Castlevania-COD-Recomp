## 4K texture pack

By **PlatinumMimic** (platinummin on Buy Me a Coffee, **u/Calm-Consideration41** on
Reddit), made for PCSX2 on the American PS2 version (SLUS-21168). The textures
were processed in chaiNNer to stay sharp at high resolutions without changing the art
style. The author distributes the pack for free, as a personal project, and accepts optional
donations. They also made the 4K pack for *Lament of Innocence*.

- Author's page (download): <https://buymeacoffee.com/platinummin/castlevania-ps2-4k-texture-packs>
- r/PCSX2 post: <https://www.reddit.com/r/PCSX2/comments/1sry8qk/castlevania_curse_of_darkness_4k_texture_pack/>
- Author's screenshots: <https://imgur.com/a/bl8rsoJ>

## What this project did with that material

- Converted texts, images and audio from the PS2 patch to the Xbox version's formats, with
  scripts that run on the user's own ISOs (`tools/ps2_*.py`). See
  [TRANSLATION.md](TRANSLATION.md).
- The game, translation, dub and HD pack files are not distributed in
  this repository.

Credits collected from the pages above on 2026-10-25. If a name or role is wrong
or missing, open an issue to fix it.

## Pacote de texturas 4K

Por **PlatinumMimic** (platinummin no Buy Me a Coffee, **u/Calm-Consideration41** no
Reddit), feito para o PCSX2 sobre a versão americana do PS2 (SLUS-21168). As texturas

opcionais. Ele também fez o pacote 4K de *Lament of Innocence*.

- Página do autor (download): <https://buymeacoffee.com/platinummin/castlevania-ps2-4k-texture-packs>
- Post no r/PCSX2: <https://www.reddit.com/r/PCSX2/comments/1sry8qk/castlevania_curse_of_darkness_4k_texture_pack/>

## O que este projeto fez com esse material

- Converteu textos, imagens e áudio do patch PS2 para os formatos da versão Xbox, por
  scripts que rodam sobre as ISOs do próprio usuário (`tools/ps2_*.py`). Veja
  [TRANSLATION.md](TRANSLATION.md#pt-br).
- Os arquivos do jogo, da tradução, da dublagem e do pacote HD não são distribuídos
  neste repositório.

Créditos levantados nas páginas acima em 25/10/2026. Se algum nome ou função estiver
